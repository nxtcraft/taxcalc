HRA Exemption Calculator — How House Rent Allowance Works

The HRA exemption calculator computes the tax-free portion of your House Rent Allowance under Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act. HRA exemption is available only under the old tax regime and only if you actually pay rent.

The 3-Condition Rule for HRA Exemption

HRA exemption is the minimum of these three amounts:

  1. Actual HRA received from your employer
  2. Actual rent paid − 10% of Basic Salary
  3. 50% of Basic Salary (metro) or 40% of Basic Salary (non-metro)

Metro vs Non-Metro for HRA

Only four cities qualify as "metro" for HRA: Mumbai, Delhi (NCR), Kolkata, and Chennai. All other cities — including Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur — are non-metro. This affects condition 3: metro employees get 50% of basic salary, non-metro employees get 40%.

Documents needed to claim HRA

  • Rent receipts (required for annual rent above ₹1 lakh)
  • PAN of landlord if annual rent exceeds ₹1 lakh
  • Rent agreement (recommended)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I claim HRA without submitting rent receipts?

If your annual rent is below ₹1 lakh (₹8,333/month), you do not need rent receipts — a self-declaration is enough. For annual rent of ₹1 lakh or more, rent receipts and the landlord's PAN are mandatory.

Can I pay rent to parents and claim HRA?

Yes — you can pay rent to your parents if they own the house and claim HRA exemption. A proper rent agreement must be signed and rent should be transferred via bank. Your parents must show this rent income in their tax return.

What if HRA is not part of my salary?

If your employer does not provide HRA but you pay rent, you can still claim deduction under Section 80GG (up to ₹60,000 per year or 25% of total income), subject to conditions. This is the alternative for self-employed individuals and those without HRA.
