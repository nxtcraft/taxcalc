Gold Tax Rules FY 2024-25

  • Physical gold: LTCG at 12.5% (held >24 months), STCG at slab rate
  • SGB maturity: Completely tax exempt if held to 8-year maturity. Premature redemption after 5 years: LTCG at 12.5%
  • Gold ETF / FoF: Treated as debt fund post-Apr 2023 — taxed at slab rate regardless of holding

Capital Gains Tax in India — Key Rules

Capital gains are profits from selling a capital asset (shares, property, gold, mutual funds). They are classified as LTCG (long-term) or STCG (short-term) based on the holding period, and taxed differently.

Budget 2024 Key Changes

  • LTCG on equity raised from 10% to 12.5%
  • STCG on equity raised from 15% to 20%
  • LTCG exemption raised from ₹1 lakh to ₹1.25 lakh
  • Indexation removed on property for sales after 23 July 2024
