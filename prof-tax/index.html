What is Professional Tax (PT)?

Professional Tax is a state-level direct tax levied by state governments on individuals earning salary, wages, or practising a profession. It is deducted by employers and remitted to the respective state government.

Maximum PT per year: ₹2,500 (as capped by Article 276 of the Indian Constitution). This cannot be exceeded by any state.

Income Tax benefit: PT paid is deductible under Section 16(iii) of the Income Tax Act — it reduces your gross salary before computing income tax. So it effectively reduces your income tax burden too.

States that have NOT levied PT: Bihar, Punjab, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Delhi, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Uttarakhand, UP, Goa, Arunachal Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, and all 7 UTs except Puducherry.
