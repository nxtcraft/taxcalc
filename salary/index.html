Frequently Asked Questions

Up to what salary is there no income tax in FY 2026-27?

Under the new regime, salary up to ₹12.75 lakh pays no income tax: the ₹75,000 standard deduction brings taxable income to ₹12 lakh, which is fully covered by the ₹60,000 Section 87A rebate.

Did Budget 2026 change the tax slabs?

No. FY 2026-27 uses the same new-regime slabs as FY 2025-26: 0% up to ₹4L, 5% to ₹8L, 10% to ₹12L, 15% to ₹16L, 20% to ₹20L, 25% to ₹24L and 30% above.
