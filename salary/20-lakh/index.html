How to Pay Less Tax on ₹20 Lakh

  • Employer NPS (80CCD(2)): ask HR to route 14% of basic (₹1,40,000/yr) to NPS. It is deductible even in the new regime and would cut your tax by about ₹29,120.
  • Check the old regime if you pay rent in a metro and have a home loan — enter both in the calculator.
  • Claim HRA and LTA only under the old regime; they are not available in the new one. HRA calculator →

Frequently Asked Questions

How much income tax is payable on ₹20 Lakh salary in FY 2026-27?

Under the new regime, tax on ₹20 Lakh salary is ₹1,92,400 (effective 9.6%), after the ₹75,000 standard deduction, including 4% cess. Under the old regime with ₹4.25 lakh of deductions it is ₹2,80,800.

What is the in-hand salary for ₹20 Lakh per annum?

Roughly ₹1,40,425 per month after new-regime tax, 12% employee EPF on a basic of 50% and ₹2,500 a year professional tax.

Which tax regime is better for ₹20 Lakh salary?

The old regime only wins if your deductions (excluding the ₹50,000 standard deduction) exceed about ₹7,10,000.
