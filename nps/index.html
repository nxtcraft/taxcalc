ℹ️ NPS Tax Rules
  • 80CCD(1): Employee contribution up to 10% of Basic+DA; counts within ₹1.5L 80C limit
  • 80CCD(1B): Additional ₹50,000 over and above 80C limit — exclusive to NPS
  • 80CCD(2): Employer contribution up to 10% of Basic+DA — allowed in both regimes
  • At maturity (60): 60% lump sum is tax-free; 40% must be used for annuity (pension), taxable as income
  • Partial withdrawal: Allowed after 3 years for specific purposes, 25% tax-free
