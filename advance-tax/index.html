ℹ️ Key Rules
  • Advance tax is mandatory if total tax liability > ₹10,000 after TDS.
  • Salaried employees fully covered by TDS generally don't need advance tax.
  • Freelancers, business income, capital gains, rental income — advance tax applies.
  • Section 234B: 1% per month interest if advance tax paid < 90% of total tax.
  • Section 234C: 1% per month interest for shortfall in each instalment.
  • Senior citizens (60+) with no business income are exempt from advance tax.
